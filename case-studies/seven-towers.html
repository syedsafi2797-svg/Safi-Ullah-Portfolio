Overview

THE PROJECT

Seven Towers is a premium real estate development by Family Builders — a residential property project requiring sustained lead generation across multiple digital channels. As the performance marketer for Family Builders, I ran end-to-end digital marketing: from campaign strategy and creative production to tracking implementation and daily optimization.

The campaign ran across Google, Meta, TikTok, Snapchat, and LinkedIn Ads simultaneously, while I also implemented GTM (Google Tag Manager) for conversion tracking, Microsoft Clarity for heatmap analysis, and keyword planner for paid search strategy.

Strategy

MULTI-PLATFORM APPROACH

PLATFORM ROLES

  • Meta Ads (Facebook & Instagram) — Video-first campaigns showcasing the tower model, progress shots, and location highlights. Click-to-WhatsApp and lead gen forms for direct inquiries
  • Google Search Ads — Intent-driven keywords: "new apartments in [city]", "buy flat", "real estate investment". Google Keyword Planner used for competitive analysis
  • YouTube Ads — Video walkthroughs and project overview ads targeting property seekers
  • LinkedIn Ads — B2B targeting of investors and business professionals interested in real estate
  • Snapchat & TikTok Ads — Younger demographic targeting with short-form video content

TRACKING & ANALYTICS

  • GTM (Google Tag Manager) — Container set up for all conversion events across platforms, ensuring accurate attribution
  • Microsoft Clarity — Heatmaps and session recordings to understand how users interacted with the landing pages and property listing site
  • GA4 — Event tracking for form submissions, button clicks, and page engagement
  • Privyr CRM — Automated lead capture from WhatsApp and form submissions directly into the CRM pipeline

WINNING CREATIVE

The "7 Towers Model Video" campaign was the standout performer — achieving 203 leads at PKR 37.53/lead from 41,526 impressions. The video creative featured a 3D model walkthrough of the tower, highlighting:

  • Modern architecture and building design
  • Location advantages and surrounding infrastructure
  • Apartment layouts and amenities
  • Construction progress and completion timeline

The "Model Video" format consistently outperformed static image ads, driving 4x more leads at a lower cost per lead.

BEYOND META

The multi-platform approach expanded reach beyond what any single channel could deliver:

  • Google Search Ads captured buyers actively searching for property — branded and non-branded keywords
  • YouTube Ads extended the video walkthrough content to property seekers on the world's largest video platform
  • LinkedIn Ads targeted real estate investors and high-net-worth professionals with a different messaging angle
  • TikTok & Snapchat built brand awareness among younger first-time buyers with short, engaging clips

All channels were unified under GTM tracking to attribute leads accurately back to their source.

The multi-platform approach brought in consistent leads week after week. The video walkthrough of the tower did exceptionally well — people could actually see what they were investing in before stepping on site.

— Campaign Performance Summary, Family Builders
