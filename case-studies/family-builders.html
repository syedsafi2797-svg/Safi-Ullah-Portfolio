Overview

CLIENT & CONTEXT

Family Builders is a real estate development company based in Pakistan. Their flagship project — Seven Towers — is a premium residential development that needed sustained lead generation across multiple digital channels. As their in-house Performance Marketer, I was responsible for the entire digital marketing engine: from campaign strategy and creative production to tracking implementation, daily optimization, and reporting.

The Seven Towers campaign ran across six platforms simultaneously: Google Search Ads, Meta Ads (Facebook & Instagram), YouTube Ads, LinkedIn Ads, TikTok Ads, and Snapchat Ads. I also implemented the full tracking stack — GTM, GA4, Meta Pixel, Microsoft Clarity for heatmaps, and Privyr CRM integration — ensuring every lead was attributed back to its source with precision.

Strategy

MULTI-PLATFORM APPROACH

PLATFORM ROLES

  • Meta Ads (Facebook & Instagram) — Video-first campaigns showcasing the 3D tower model, construction progress, and location highlights. Click-to-WhatsApp and lead gen forms for direct inquiries
  • Google Search Ads — Intent-driven keywords: "new apartments", "buy flat", "real estate investment" using Google Keyword Planner for competitive analysis
  • YouTube Ads — Extended video walkthroughs and project overview ads targeting property seekers
  • LinkedIn Ads — B2B targeting of real estate investors and high-net-worth professionals
  • TikTok & Snapchat Ads — Younger demographic with short-form video content for first-time buyers

TRACKING & ANALYTICS

  • GTM (Google Tag Manager) — Container built for all conversion events across platforms, ensuring accurate attribution
  • Microsoft Clarity — Heatmaps and session recordings to understand how users interacted with landing pages and property listing site
  • GA4 — Event tracking for form submissions, button clicks, page engagement, and cross-platform attribution
  • Privyr CRM — Automated lead capture from WhatsApp messages and form submissions directly into CRM pipeline
  • Keyword Planner — Competitive analysis for paid search strategy and organic opportunities

WINNING CREATIVE

The "7 Towers Model Video" campaign was the standout performer — generating 203 leads at PKR 37.53/lead from 41,526 impressions. The creative featured a 3D model walkthrough of the tower, highlighting:

  • Modern architecture and building design
  • Location advantages and surrounding infrastructure
  • Apartment layouts and amenities
  • Construction progress and completion timeline

The video format consistently outperformed static image ads, driving 4x more leads at a lower cost per lead. The same video creative was repurposed across YouTube and TikTok for extended reach.

BEYOND META

The multi-platform approach expanded reach beyond what any single channel could deliver:

  • Google Search Ads captured buyers actively searching for property with branded and non-branded keywords
  • YouTube Ads extended the video walkthrough content to property seekers on the world's largest video platform
  • LinkedIn Ads targeted real estate investors and high-net-worth professionals with a tailored message
  • TikTok & Snapchat built brand awareness among younger first-time buyers with short, engaging clips

All channels were unified under GTM tracking to attribute every lead accurately back to its source platform — no black boxes, full visibility into what was working.

Analysis

KEY INSIGHTS

WHAT WORKED

  • Video walkthroughs outperformed static images — The 3D model video creative generated 203 leads (54% of all leads) at the lowest CPL of PKR 37.53
  • Campaign iteration improved results — Original video campaign was duplicated and optimized, with the second iteration still generating 110 leads at a slightly higher but acceptable CPL
  • Fresh campaign launches with video — The "21 July" launch achieved PKR 38.90 CPL, close to the best performer, showing the video format's reliability

OPTIMIZATION LEARNINGS

  • Major changes hurt performance — The "with Changes" variant saw CPL spike to PKR 177.47 with only 2 leads, highlighting the importance of iterative vs. overhaul changes
  • Brand awareness campaigns — "7 Towers Family Builders" (3,374 impressions) served as top-of-funnel without direct lead generation, feeding retargeting pools
  • Lead Gen format underperformed — The native Lead Gen form campaign (158 impressions, 0 leads) suggests the Click-to-WhatsApp format was strongly preferred by this audience

The video walkthrough of the Seven Towers did exceptionally well — people could actually see what they were investing in before stepping on site. And with tracking on every platform, we knew exactly what was driving results.
