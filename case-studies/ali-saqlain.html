Overview

CLIENT & CONTEXT

Ali Saqlain is a real estate development company in Pakistan, marketing residential plots, villas (152–500 sq yd), commercial properties, and project developments including Jinnah Heights, 5th Avenue, Downtown, Sports City, Precinct 32, and TGR Commercial.

As the in-house Performance Marketer, I was responsible for planning, executing, and optimizing all Meta Ads (Facebook & Instagram) lead generation campaigns — from audience targeting and creative production to daily optimization and reporting. Over the featured period (June 2024 – February 2025), I ran 30 unique campaigns across the company's full property portfolio, managing a total ad spend of PKR 1,882,908.

Strategy

APPROACH & TACTICS

CAMPAIGN STRUCTURE

  • Product-Based Campaigns — Each property project (Jinnah Heights, 5th Avenue, Downtown, Sports City, Precinct 32) ran as its own campaign with dedicated ad sets, audiences, and creative
  • Merged Campaigns — After proving individual project models, combined Jinnah Heights + 5th Avenue into one campaign to scale budget while maintaining efficiency
  • Lead Form Ads — Mobile-optimized lead forms as primary conversion method for low-friction inquiries
  • Click-to-WhatsApp — Secondary conversion path for warm leads requiring consultation

OPTIMIZATION

  • Audience Layering — Interest-based targeting for real estate buyers combined with lookalikes from past converters
  • Creative Rotation — Refreshed ad copy and imagery every 2 weeks to maintain CTR and prevent fatigue
  • Budget Reallocation — Shifted spend toward best-performing campaigns weekly, scaling winners and cutting underperformers
  • Geo-Targeting — Specific city and neighborhood targeting for each project to maximize relevance
  • Seasonal Timing — Aligned campaign launches with property launch announcements and seasonal buyer activity

Insights

WHAT THE DATA SHOWED

KEY LEARNINGS

  • Jinnah is the star brand — 59% of all leads came from Jinnah-related campaigns. This project line has the strongest market appeal
  • Lead forms beat video for direct conversions — Video campaigns (Sports City) had 5–7× higher CPL than static lead form ads. Video is best as top-of-funnel content
  • Merged campaigns scale well — Combining two projects under one campaign structure allowed 2× budget scaling without loss of efficiency
  • Summer peaked, winter dipped — June was the highest-volume month (970 leads); November saw a sharp drop (76 leads) — seasonal patterns are real in Pakistan real estate

RECOMMENDATIONS

  • Revive Jinnah Heights standalone — PKR 228 CPL is the proven formula; relaunch with fresh creative
  • Scale Precinct 32's formula — PKR 77 CPL is exceptional; test at 5× budget to confirm scalability
  • Refresh creatives quarterly — The Jun → Nov volume decline suggests creative fatigue, not audience saturation
  • Run video as awareness layer + retarget with lead forms — Use video for reach, then convert the engaged audience with lead form ads
