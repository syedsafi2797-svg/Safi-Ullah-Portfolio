Overview

CLIENT & CONTEXT

Anytime Fitness is the world's largest co-ed fitness franchise. I managed Meta Ads for two US locations — Fairfield and Simi Valley — for 1.5 years, running campaigns across lead generation, video engagement, and awareness objectives. The goal was consistent: drive membership sign-ups and trial visits at a sustainable cost per lead.

I ran 13 campaigns across both locations including lead form ads, video retargeting, awareness campaigns, and tailored follow-up campaigns based on audience behavior.

Insights

KEY HIGHLIGHTS

WHAT WORKED

  • Video ads drove the best CPL — Fairfield Video Campaign hit $7.20 CPL, the most efficient of all campaigns
  • Best converting campaign — Video Ad Campaign (both locations) each generated 29 leads at ~$10–11 CPL
  • Awareness + retargeting funnel — Ran top-of-funnel reach campaigns ($5.90 CPM) alongside lead gen ads, capturing engaged users and retargeting them

LOCATION COMPARISON

  • Fairfield consistently outperformed Simi Valley on CPL ($12.83 vs $14.25) — tighter audience targeting
  • Simi Valley still delivered strong volume at $14.25 CPL — healthy lead flow for a second location
  • Combined campaigns (both locations under one ad set) balanced out performance and simplified management
