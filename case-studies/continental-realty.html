Overview

CLIENT & CONTEXT

Continental Realty is a real estate brokerage based in Pakistan. They were selling units in the Seven Towers development (by Family Builders) and needed a strong social media presence to showcase available properties, generate inquiries, and build trust with potential buyers.

I was responsible for their social media marketing — creating posts, reels, and Meta ad campaigns designed to drive property inquiries. The strategy involved showcasing properties through high-quality visual content, highlighting location advantages and unit features, and running targeted Meta Ads to reach potential home buyers and investors interested in Seven Towers.

Strategy

CONTENT & AD APPROACH

CONTENT STRATEGY

  • Property Showcase Posts — High-quality images of Seven Towers units with key selling points highlighted
  • Location Spotlight Reels — Short videos showing the property location, nearby amenities, and neighborhood highlights
  • Client Testimonials — Social proof content featuring satisfied buyers and their experiences with Continental Realty
  • Deal & Offer Posts — Limited-time promotions and payment plan highlights to create urgency

AD CAMPAIGNS

  • Meta Ads (Facebook & Instagram) — Targeted campaigns reaching potential property buyers based on location, income level, and real estate interest
  • Lead Generation Funnels — Click-to-WhatsApp and lead form campaigns for direct property inquiries
  • Retargeting — Following up with users who engaged with Seven Towers content but didn't inquire
  • Seasonal Campaigns — Special promotions tied to holiday seasons and real estate market cycles
