Overview

CLIENT & CONTEXT

Grilly's Burger & Pizza is a Karachi-based restaurant known for premium burgers, loaded fries, pizzas, and indulgent combo deals. They needed a consistent digital presence driving direct orders — not just engagement metrics.

I managed their Meta Ads (Facebook & Instagram) with Click-to-WhatsApp funnels and produced all their social media content — posts, reels, and promotional graphics. The goal was simple: turn ad spend into direct WhatsApp conversations leading to orders.

Strategy

APPROACH

AD STRATEGY

  • Click-to-WhatsApp Funnel — Every ad directs to WhatsApp with a pre-filled order message, minimizing friction
  • Deal-Focused Creatives — Highlighted specific deals: Smokey Zinger Combo, Treat Time deals, seasonal promotions
  • Location Targeting — Geo-targeted around Karachi with interest layering for food lovers
  • Messaging Conversations — Optimized for conversation starts rather than link clicks, ensuring real engagement

CONTENT PRODUCTION

  • Static Posts — High-quality food photography with deal overlays, pricing, and CTAs
  • Reels — Short-form video showing food prep, sizzling shots, and meal unboxing
  • Promotional Graphics — Deal cards, combo menus, and limited-time offers
  • Brand Consistency — All content follows Grilly's brand colors and visual style
