Overview

CLIENT & CONTEXT

Ostruce Oil is an e-commerce brand selling oil products directly to consumers. They needed a consistent, conversion-focused Meta Ads program that turned cold traffic into website purchases — not just engagement or page likes.

I ran their account as a long-term performance program (campaigns live since Nov 2024, many still ongoing), building a full funnel: UGC video creatives for cold audiences, website purchase campaigns for direct sales, WhatsApp ad sets for conversation-led conversion, and Lookalike (LAL) audiences built from the best-performing customers to scale what worked.

Strategy

CAMPAIGN ARCHITECTURE

SALES ENGINE

  • Dr UGC x New Sales — Doctor-endorsed UGC video content driving website purchases, the account's largest spend allocation (Rs 40,859.62) for direct response sales
  • New Sales Campaign - Video — Video-first sales campaigns built around product demonstration and benefit-led storytelling
  • Winning Creative + LAL — The proven top creative amplified through Lookalike audiences, generating 121 website purchases
  • New Sales Campaign — Core conversion campaigns targeting high-intent shoppers with purchase-optimized delivery

CONVERSION & RETARGETING

  • WhatsApp Insta Feed only — Instagram feed ads with Click-to-WhatsApp CTA, generating 91+ messaging conversations from warm buyers
  • New Engagement ad set — Engagement-optimized sets feeding the retargeting pool and warming cold traffic
  • Purchase event tracking — Website purchase conversions measured and optimized through the Meta pixel, with data feeding LAL audience building
  • Creative iteration — UGC and video variants continuously refreshed to fight ad fatigue and protect cost per purchase

WHAT DROVE SALES

The "Dr UGC x New Sales" campaign was the account's sales engine — the largest budget allocation (Rs 40,859.62) spent on doctor-endorsed UGC video that built trust and converted cold traffic into website purchases.

The "Winning Creative + LAL" campaign was the efficiency play: by feeding the proven creative into Lookalike audiences built from existing customers, it generated 121 website purchases — the highest single-campaign result in the account — at a fraction of the top campaign's budget.

WHY THIS WORKED

  • Trust-first creatives — Doctor UGC positioned the product as credible, removing purchase hesitation
  • Funnel separation — Dedicated campaigns for purchases vs. WhatsApp conversations vs. engagement, each optimized for its own event
  • Lookalike scaling — LAL audiences turned the best customers into a scalable acquisition channel
  • Long-run consistency — Campaigns ran continuously (some since Nov 2024), compounding data and pixel learning
