Overview

CLIENT & CONTEXT

The Luxury Escape is a Pakistan-based travel agency specializing in Umrah packages, Far East tours, holiday packages, airline ticketing, and visa services. Drawing on deep travel industry experience, I manage their Meta Ads (Facebook & Instagram) and create all social media content.

The agency needed consistent lead generation through Click-to-WhatsApp and Messaging conversations — the primary conversion channel for Pakistani travel buyers. Over 12 months, I ran 56 campaigns targeting different travel segments throughout the seasonal calendar.

Strategy

CAMPAIGN STRATEGY

SEASONAL PACKAGE FOCUS

Each campaign targeted a specific travel product and season:

  • Umrah Packages — Year-round with peaks during Ramadan and off-peak seasons (Shaheer, Razi, Hania-named campaigns)
  • Holiday Packages — Bali, Turkey, Far East tours for leisure travelers
  • Honeymoon Campaign — Video-driven romantic getaway promotions
  • Visa Services — Umrah visa campaigns for DIY travelers
  • Airline Tickets — Competitive fare promotions
  • Ramadan Specials — Group Umrah packages during holy month

CREATIVE & TARGETING

Mix of image and video creatives tailored to each package type:

  • Image campaigns — Deal cards, package highlights, price-focused creatives for quick lead capture
  • Video campaigns — Destination teasers, tour experience reels, emotional storytelling for Umrah
  • Carousel campaigns — Multi-destination showcases, package feature breakdowns
  • Influencer-style — Named individuals (Shaheer, Razi, Hania) as recognizable faces for trust-building

All campaigns used Click-to-WhatsApp as the primary conversion goal — the preferred booking channel for Pakistani travelers.

Analysis

KEY INSIGHTS

WHAT WORKED BEST

  • Image + Video Mix — Campaigns using both media types consistently outperformed single-format campaigns, achieving an average CPR of PKR 13.60 vs PKR 24-60 for image-only
  • Specific Package Targeting — Named campaigns (Shaheer, Razi, Hania) built recognizable brand faces and drove higher trust, with the Shaheer & Razi Umrah campaign generating 1,511 conversations
  • Led by influencers/staff — Using real people in creatives outperformed generic stock imagery
  • Re-targeting worked — The "New Leads Campaign" iterations showed progressively better CPR as audiences warmed up

OPTIMIZATION LEARNINGS

  • Video for Honeymoon & Experience — The Honeymoon Campaign (video-only) achieved PKR 21.78 CPR with 523 conversations, proving video drives emotional bookings
  • Carousels for Package Comparison — The Carousel Campaign achieved an impressive PKR 8.76 CPR with 532 conversations, excellent for multi-destination inventory
  • Airline & Visa as Loss Leaders — Airline ticket (PKR 5.49) and visa campaigns (PKR 13.29) had the lowest CPRs, suggesting these entry-point offers convert easily
  • Active vs Archived — Active campaigns averaged PKR 24.78 CPR vs archived PKR 15.89, indicating ongoing optimization beats starting fresh

Over 12 months and 56 campaigns, the most efficient strategy was clear: run image-and-video combos with real brand faces, target specific packages in seasonal windows, and use Click-to-WhatsApp as the conversion mechanism.
